Map — Floor 1

Raycasting Mini-Doom

A grid of walls turned into a pseudo-3D corridor view, the same technique Wolfenstein 3D and the original DOOM used: cast one ray per screen column, and however far it travels before hitting a wall decides how tall that column's wall slice is drawn. Ten floors, each randomly generated the moment the page loads.

WASD or Arrow Keys to move and turn. Space or Click to fire. E to use a staircase.
